OpenSpec Proposal — Business Invitations
Domain: Business Invitations
Actors

Business Owner: A User who owns a Business

Invitee: A person identified by invitedEmail (may or may not already have a User account)

Invitation Lifecycle
Invitation States
PENDING → ACCEPTED
PENDING → EXPIRED (optional, future)

Rules & Constraints
Creation

Only a Business Owner may create invitations for their business

Invitation must include:

invitedEmail

role (ADMIN or MEMBER; OWNER is forbidden)

token is:

Automatically generated by the system

Cryptographically secure

Globally unique

An invitation may be created regardless of whether the invitee already exists as a User

A business may not have more than one active (PENDING) invitation for the same invitedEmail

Visibility & Access Control
Actor	Action	Allowed
Business Owner	Create invitation	✅
Business Owner	View invitations	✅ (own business only)
Business Owner	Update invitation	✅ (role only, if PENDING)
Business Owner	Delete invitation	✅ (if PENDING)
Invitee (via token)	View invitation	✅
Invitee (via token)	Accept invitation	✅
Invitee (via token)	Update / Delete	❌
Any other user	Any action	❌
Viewing by Token

Anyone supplying a valid invitation token may:

View invitation details

Accept the invitation

Token-based access bypasses authentication, but:

Only applies to that single invitation

Grants no other system access

Acceptance

An invitation may be accepted only if:

Status is PENDING

Token is valid

Upon acceptance:

A User is resolved:

If a User with invitedEmail exists → use it

Otherwise → user must register first, then resume acceptance

A BusinessUser entry is created:

business = invitation.business

user = resolved User

role = invitation.role

Invitation status is set to ACCEPTED

Invitation becomes immutable

Post-Acceptance Rules

ACCEPTED invitations:

Cannot be updated

Cannot be deleted

Are read-only for auditing purposes

Re-inviting the same email is allowed only after previous invitation is ACCEPTED or DELETED

API Intent (High-Level)
POST   /api/businesses/{id}/invitations        (owner only)
GET    /api/businesses/{id}/invitations        (owner only)
DELETE /api/invitations/{id}                   (owner only, PENDING)

GET    /api/invitations/by-token/{token}       (public)
POST   /api/invitations/{token}/accept         (authenticated)
