# Business Access Controls - User Guide

## Overview
This guide explains the new access controls that restrict what businesses you can see and manage in the system.

## What Changed?

Previously, you might have been able to see all businesses in the system. With the new access controls:

- You can now only see businesses that you are a member of
- You can only edit or delete businesses that you own
- Other users' businesses are hidden from your view

## Understanding Business Roles

### Business Members
As a business member, you can:
- View the business details
- See other members of the business

### Business Owners
As a business owner, you can:
- View the business details
- Edit the business information
- Delete the business
- Manage business members

## What You Can Expect

### Business Listing
- When you visit the business listing page, you'll only see businesses you're a member of
- If you're not a member of any businesses, the list will be empty

### Business Details
- You can view details for any business you're a member of
- If you try to access a business you're not a member of, you'll see an error message

### Editing Businesses
- You'll only see "Edit" buttons for businesses you own
- If you try to edit a business you don't own, you'll receive an access denied message

### Deleting Businesses
- You'll only see "Delete" buttons for businesses you own
- If you try to delete a business you don't own, you'll receive an access denied message

## Common Scenarios

### Scenario 1: Viewing Your Businesses
1. Log in to the system
2. Navigate to the "Businesses" section
3. You'll see a list of businesses you're a member of
4. If you own any of these businesses, you'll see "Edit" and "Delete" options

### Scenario 2: Trying to Access Another User's Business
1. If you have a direct link to a business you're not a member of
2. You'll receive an "Access Denied" error
3. Contact the business owner if you believe you should have access

### Scenario 3: Creating a New Business
1. When you create a new business, you automatically become the owner
2. You'll have full access to edit and manage this business
3. Other users will need to be added as members to access it

## Need Help?

If you believe you should have access to a business but cannot see it or manage it:

1. Contact the business owner to confirm your membership status
2. If you're the intended owner of a business, ask the current owner to transfer ownership to you
3. Contact your system administrator if you need further assistance

## Troubleshooting

### Problem: I can't see a business I used to see
**Solution:** Business access is now limited to members only. Contact the business owner to be added as a member.

### Problem: I can't edit a business I helped create
**Solution:** Only the business owner can edit business details. If you should be the owner, contact the current owner to transfer ownership.

### Problem: Edit/Delete buttons are missing
**Solution:** These buttons only appear for businesses you own. If you believe you should own a business, contact the current owner.
